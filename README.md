# Vein Mining — Break Entire Ore Veins Instantly

Vein Mining 9minecraft Mod adds a special enchantment that lets you break a whole connected group of blocks at once. When you mine one block of coal or iron with your enchanted tool, the rest of the matching blocks pop out together. This mod saves you time and stops you from having to hit every single ore block in a dark cave. It works on many materials like ores and logs, making resource gathering much faster while keeping normal survival rules intact.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **C4**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
